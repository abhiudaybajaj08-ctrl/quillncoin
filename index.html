Questions

Before you pay

Who prepares my plans?

Student analysts trained in portfolio and wealth management work with you on your profile. Your deck is prepared from the details you provide.

What do you ask for in the form?

Your income, regular commitments, savings, goals, time horizon and how you feel about risk. These are what we need to build a risk profile and decide how your plans are ranked.

Do I have to invest after getting the deck?

No. The deck is yours to keep. If you want to go ahead with a plan, we connect you with Nuvama Wealth. That step is your choice.

Is this a guarantee of returns?

No. Every plan shows its risk next to its expected return, and markets can move against any plan. Investments are subject to market risks.

Can the intern take payment from me?

No. The ₹99 fee is paid only through this page. Interns never collect money from clients.
